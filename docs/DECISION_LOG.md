# 🔱 Trinity — Decision Log

This document records important Trinity decisions and the reasoning behind them.

---

## Decision #001

### Date

2026-09-25

### Decision

Begin Trinity with problem discovery and validation rather than immediate product development.

### Reason

Building a cybersecurity product before validating the underlying problem creates a significant risk of solving a problem users do not have or existing products already solve sufficiently.

### Consequence

No major MVP development will begin until initial research produces sufficient evidence.

### Status

Accepted

---

## Decision #002

### Date

2026-09-25

### Decision

Use college students and young internet users in India as the initial research population.

### Reason

They are accessible for early user research and have substantial exposure to digital services.

### Important limitation

This does not establish them as Trinity's final target market.

### Status

Accepted

---

## Decision #003

### Date

2026-09-25

### Decision

Do not build a proprietary SLM during the initial phase.

### Reason

A specialized model should be justified by technical and product requirements rather than built simply because Trinity is an AI cybersecurity project.

### Status

Accepted

---

## Decision #004

### Date

2026-09-25

### Decision

Do not build the full Trinity platform immediately.

### Reason

The first objective is to identify and validate one specific, high-value cybersecurity problem.

### Status

Accepted
